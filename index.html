About me!

I am at Alibaba Holding, which I joined in August 2026. My research focuses on large language model agents and reinforcement learning. Previously, I worked at Alibaba International from July 2024 to July 2026, applying agent research to customer service, voice interaction, and multimodal decision-making.

I received my master's degree in Intelligent Control from the School of Intelligent Systems Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University in 2024, and my bachelor's degree in Computational Materials from the same university in 2021. Previously, I interned at Tencent AI Lab and Alibaba International.

My research interests include LLM agents, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and multimodal learning. I am particularly interested in learning from multi-turn interactions, aligning agents with human preferences, and improving their reasoning and tool use.
